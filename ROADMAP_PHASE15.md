# Roadmap Phase 15: Cash Desk, Travel Expenses & Blockchain Audit Ledger

## Proposed Next Milestones (Phase 15)

### M54: Autonomous Cash Desk & Petty Cash Management Engine (Сметка 501 / ПКО & РКО)
- **Objective**: Automate cash register operations, cash receipt orders (ПКО) and cash payment orders (РКО).
- **Key Features**:
  - Cash ledger entries (Debit 501 / Credit 411 or Credit 501 / Debit 401).
  - Daily cash desk limit monitoring and cash discrepancy reporting.

### M55: Autonomous Business Travel Expenses & Per Diem Allowance Manager (Сметка 609 / Командировъчни)
- **Objective**: Automate domestic and international business travel expense processing under Bulgarian travel regulations (Наредба за командировките).
- **Key Features**:
  - Daily per diem and accommodation expense journal entries (Debit 609 / Credit 422 / Credit 501).
  - Statutory travel order document generation.

### M56: Automated Financial Audit Trail & Tamper-Evident Blockchain Ledger Integration
- **Objective**: Provide tamper-evident cryptographic audit trailing for all accounting mutations.
- **Key Features**:
  - Immutable SHA-256 hash chaining of journal entries.
  - Verification of historical ledger integrity.
