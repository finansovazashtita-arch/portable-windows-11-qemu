# Cognitive Accounting & Global Compliance Roadmap - Phase 5 (Post-M18 Milestones)

## 📌 Context
With Milestones M1 through M18 100% completed, verified (190/190 passing tests), containerized, clustered, and integrated across `macmini-primary`, `macmini-secondary`, and the Windows 11 QEMU VM, the following Phase 5 initiatives are proposed for future system evolution:

---

## 🎯 Proposed Milestones

### 1. **M19: Real-Time Cash Flow Forecasting & Financial Health Engine**
- **Scope**: ML liquidity forecasting model utilizing historic bank statements and double-entry accounting journals to predict 30/60/90-day cash flow trends and upcoming tax liabilities.
- **Benefit**: Empowers business executives with forward-looking liquidity intelligence.

### 2. **M20: Automated SAF-T (Standard Audit File for Tax) & NRA/НАП Export**
- **Scope**: Compliance exporter producing OECD SAF-T XML files and Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (НАП) audit files.
- **Benefit**: Ensures 100% readiness for national tax audits and regulatory compliance.

### 3. **M21: Multi-Currency FX Real-Time Revaluation Engine**
- **Scope**: BNB/ECB official exchange rate integration for real-time foreign currency statement processing and foreign exchange gain/loss accounting entries (Accounts 624 / 724).
- **Benefit**: Seamless support for multi-currency corporate bank accounts in USD, GBP, CHF.

### 4. **M22: Mobile Notification & Telegram Audit Bot Guard**
- **Scope**: Instant mobile & Telegram bot notification alerts triggered whenever critical fraud flags or VM discrepancies occur.
- **Benefit**: Provides immediate alerts to CFOs and chief accountants on high-risk events.
