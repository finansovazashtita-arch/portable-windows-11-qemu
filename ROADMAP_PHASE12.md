# Roadmap Phase 12: Regulatory Intelligence & Cross-Border EU E-Commerce

## Proposed Next Milestones (Phase 12)

### M45: Autonomous Tax Policy & Regulatory Update Ingestion Engine
- **Objective**: Automate the monitoring of Bulgarian State Gazette (Държавен вестник) and NRA (НАП) tax law modifications.
- **Key Features**:
  - Web scraping and NLP analysis of tax law changes (ZDDTS / ZKPO / ZDDFL).
  - Dynamic adjustments to account mapping rules and tax rate thresholds.

### M46: Intelligent AI Voice Assistant & Hands-Free Accounting Query Interface
- **Objective**: Enable hands-free voice query processing for accountants asking for real-time financial metrics.
- **Key Features**:
  - Bulgarian voice recognition (STT) and synthesized speech responses (TTS).
  - Voice queries for account balances, cash flow forecasts, and missing invoice alerts.

### M47: Autonomous Cross-Border EU Tax & OSS (One-Stop-Shop) E-Commerce Invoicing Adapter
- **Objective**: Automate cross-border EU e-commerce VAT accounting under the One-Stop-Shop (OSS) scheme.
- **Key Features**:
  - Multi-country EU VAT rate calculation.
  - Quarterly OSS declaration generation and double-entry mapping for EU B2C sales.
