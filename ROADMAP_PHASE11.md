# Roadmap Phase 11: Enterprise Banking Settlement & Autonomous Tax Defense

## Proposed Next Milestones (Phase 11)

### M42: Multi-Bank Instant Payment Gateway & SEPA Instant / BISERA Integration Adapter
- **Objective**: Automate real-time SEPA Instant and BISERA 6 bank payment settlement ingestion and direct payment initiation.
- **Key Features**:
  - Sub-second payment confirmation streaming via Open Banking PSD2.
  - Automatic reconciliation between bank instant transfer receipts and pending accounts payable (Account 401).

### M43: Autonomous Tax Audit Defense & Discrepancy Risk Scoring Engine
- **Objective**: Provide automated NRA tax audit risk assessment evaluating potential audit triggers before filing.
- **Key Features**:
  - Machine learning scoring for VAT discrepancies (Art. 92 VATA / Чл. 92 ЗДДС).
  - Automated detection of missing tax invoices or counterparty VIES deregistration risk.

### M44: Multi-Language Executive Financial Briefing & Audio Narrative Generator
- **Objective**: Produce automated daily executive C-level financial summary reports in Bulgarian, English, and German.
- **Key Features**:
  - Executive financial summary Markdown reports.
  - Multi-lingual key performance indicators (KPIs) for C-level decision makers.
