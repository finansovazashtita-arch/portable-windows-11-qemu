# Roadmap Phase 13: Enterprise Inventory, Fixed Assets & Corporate Consolidation

## Proposed Next Milestones (Phase 13)

### M48: Autonomous Enterprise Inventory & Stock Valuation Engine
- **Objective**: Automate inventory double-entry accounting and stock valuation.
- **Key Features**:
  - Support for FIFO and Weighted Average cost valuation methods.
  - Automated journal entries for inventory receipts (304), write-offs (601), and cost of goods sold (702).

### M49: Automated Depreciation & Fixed Assets Schedule Manager
- **Objective**: Manage tangible/intangible fixed assets and monthly depreciation calculation.
- **Key Features**:
  - Bulgarian NRA tax categories I-VII fixed asset registration.
  - Automatic monthly depreciation journal entries (Debit 603 / Credit 241).

### M50: Autonomous Multi-Entity Corporate Consolidation & Intercompany Elimination Engine
- **Objective**: Consolidate multi-subsidiary financial statements and eliminate intercompany balances.
- **Key Features**:
  - Consolidated Balance Sheet and Income Statement generation.
  - Automated elimination of intercompany receivables (411) and payables (401).
