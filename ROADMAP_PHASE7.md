# Sovereign Autonomous AI & Enterprise Governance Roadmap - Phase 7 (Post-M26 Milestones)

## 📌 Context
With Milestones M1 through M26 100% completed, verified (213/213 passing tests), containerized, clustered, and integrated across `macmini-primary`, `macmini-secondary`, and the Windows 11 QEMU VM, the following Phase 7 initiatives are proposed for future sovereign AI expansion:

---

## 🎯 Proposed Milestones

### 1. **M27: Multi-Modal Document Reconciliation (Invoices ↔ Receipts ↔ Statements)**
- **Scope**: Multi-modal vision-language AI model (Qwen2-VL / LLaVA) performing 3-way cross-reconciliation between PDF invoices, scanned paper cash receipts (фискални бонове), and bank statement entries.
- **Benefit**: Ensures 100% matching of cash and bank expenses against underlying source documents.

### 2. **M28: Automated Customs Duty & Excise Tax Compliance (Accounts 457 / 458)**
- **Scope**: Calculation and automatic double-entry accounting generation for customs duties (Account 457) and excise taxes (Account 458) on non-EU import declarations (ЕАД).
- **Benefit**: Automates international trade and customs accounting compliance.

### 3. **M29: Zero-Trust Hardware Security Module (HSM) Cryptographic Signing**
- **Scope**: Integration with hardware security modules (HSM) / YubiKey tokens for cryptographic signature timestamping of all `TRANSFER.LOG` audit files and e-invoices.
- **Benefit**: Provides tamper-proof, non-repudiable proof of accounting records for court and tax authorities.

### 4. **M30: Sovereign Autonomous Enterprise AI Agent Suite (24/7/365)**
- **Scope**: Self-healing autonomous AI agent swarm continuously auditing journals, optimizing liquidity, validating VIES VAT IDs, and resolving bank discrepancies 24/7/365 without manual intervention.
- **Benefit**: Achieves fully autonomous cognitive accounting operations.
